# Acme Retail — Data Platform Onboarding

Welcome to Acme Retail's data platform. Your Trino access covers the
`sales` schema (and `hr` if you're in `grp_hr`); your Airflow access
covers `sales_etl`, `hr_payroll_sync`, or `customer_export` depending on
your grant. Ask a workspace admin if something looks missing — but it
will never be org-002's data, no matter who asks.
